## Decisions (human-reviewed)

| Task | Model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| parse | Haiku 4.5 | Best accuracy per dollar among top scorers; Qwen3 32B (0.913) is the cheap runner-up. At ~50 calls/week the difference is pennies. |
| slack | **Nova Lite** → Haiku fallback | 0.992, same as Haiku at 1/21 the cost. Slow (~5s) but it's an admin-only tool. |
| slip | Haiku 4.5 → Sonnet 4.6 fallback | Money is on the line; Sonnet also re-reads low-confidence tickets. Rendered tickets are cleaner than real photos — re-run with real slips. |
| fun | **Qwen3 Next 80B** → Haiku fallback | The automatic pick (Ministral 8B) produced flat names; Qwen3 Next's were specific and funny in human review, at 1/8 Haiku's cost. 3 cases is too few for the judge score alone. |

Not usable today:
- **Llama 4 Scout/Maverick:** intermittent `ResourceNotFoundException: Access denied` via the cross-region profile.
- **Gemma 3 27B, gpt-oss-120b:** often skip required fields.
- **Nova Micro:** frequent invalid tool-use sequences.

The run-1 Slack numbers were wrong: the schema field `playerName` was ambiguous, so models filled it with athletes, and word-overlap grading missed equivalent bets. Both are fixed in this run.
